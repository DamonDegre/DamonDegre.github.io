# 30 September 2026
After some thinking, I have decided to pivot to a pivot. Rather than stay stationary on the backpack and individually unlatch cassettes, I will be able to unlatch the entire case and take out a cassette easier. This will reduce some aspects of the complexity and should make it easier to choose a cassette while on the move. The moment created from this may be too strong for the plastic so testing will be done.
